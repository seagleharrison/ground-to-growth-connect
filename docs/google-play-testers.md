# Getting your 12 testers (needed for a public Google Play release)

Google's rule for personal accounts: **at least 12 testers must stay opted in to the closed test for 14 days in a row.** Plan for 15 to 20 people, because some will forget or drop out.

## Who can be a tester
- Anyone with an **Android phone** and a **Google (Gmail) account**. iPhone users can't test the Android app.
- They give you the **Gmail address** they use on that phone. You add those addresses to the tester list in Play Console.
- Good testers: volunteers, staff, board members, friends, family, a few people you serve who have an Android phone and are comfortable helping.
- Tell them it's a **test**: please don't enter real documents. Names and phone numbers can be real or made up.

## Message to send (text or email)
> Hi, it's Harrison from Ground to Growth. We've built an app to help the people we serve with rides, supplies, and keeping documents safe, and I need 12 friendly people with Android phones to try it for two weeks before we release it publicly.
>
> It takes about 2 minutes to set up and a minute or two a few times over the next 2 weeks to open the app. Could you help?
>
> If yes, please reply with the Gmail address you use on your Android phone. I'll send you a link to install it from the Play Store.
>
> One important thing: please keep the app installed for the full 14 days. Google needs it to count.

## After you add them: message with the link
> Thanks! Here's your link: [paste the opt-in link]
> 1. Open it on your Android phone and tap **Become a tester**.
> 2. Tap **Download it on Google Play** and install.
> 3. Open the app and sign up (name and phone number; "Getting support" is fine).
> 4. Please keep it installed for 14 days, and open it a few times. Tell me anything that looks broken or confusing.
> Thank you!

## Keep track
Make a simple list: name, Gmail, date they accepted. Check in with everyone around day 7. If someone drops, replace them straight away. The 14 days only count while you have 12 or more.

## What testers might hit (so you aren't surprised)
- Notifications don't arrive on Android yet.
- Location sharing asks them to choose "Allow all the time" for background sharing. It's optional for testing.
